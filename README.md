#The Superman Archive

**A comprehensive encyclopedia dedicated to the world of Superman.

The Superman Archive is a planned open-source web application dedicated to documenting and exploring the history, characters, stories, and universe of Superman.

Inspired by the idea of a dedicated Superman Wikipedia, the project aims to create an interconnected knowledge base for fans, readers, and newcomers alike.

##Planned Features
- **Character Encyclopedia — Explore Superman, allies, enemies, and supporting characters.
- **Comics & Storylines — Discover important comic runs, major events, and reading orders.
- **Powers & Abilities — Explore Superman’s abilities, weaknesses, and their variations across different continuities.
- **Locations & Objects — Learn about Krypton, the Fortress of Solitude, Kryptonite, and other iconic elements.
- **Adaptations — Explore Superman across comics, films, and television, including Smallville.
- **Search & Discovery — Search entries and navigate relationships between characters, stories, places, and events.

##Project Goals
-Build a polished, accessible, and searchable knowledge base.
-Organise information through interconnected entries and structured data.
-Provide clear distinctions between different Superman continuities and adaptations.
-Develop a useful, free resource for the Superman community.
